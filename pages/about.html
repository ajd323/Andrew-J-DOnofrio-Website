Proud Italian-American · Electrical apprentice · Jazz guitarist (6+ years) · Mets enthusiast

I'm a recent mechanical engineering graduate from Cornell University, born and raised on Long Island, New York. My fascination with the intersection of consumer products and healthcare started with the first EpiPen I carried as a child for severe allergies. That early, visceral relationship with a medical device planted the seed for everything that followed.

Today, I'm passionate about integrating experience in mechanical design and prototyping into not only biotechnology products, but also consumer devices that live at the intersection of all fields of research.

I'm a proud first-generation student from a family of business owners and an electrical apprentice from as early as I can remember. I believe that background and cultural experience fundamentally shape how we interact with products. That conviction drives me to bring personal perspectives to medical device design and to listen actively to others' stories.

Looking ahead, I'm excited to pursue a career at the crossroads of R&D, product development, and manufacturing especially in MedTech. My ambitions include leading complex engineering teams, deepening expertise in quality and regulatory standards, and finding opportunities with genuine creative freedom.
